Accessible

Before you
come in.

Yes. Walk-ins are always welcome and we will seat you as soon as a chair opens. That said, appointments are recommended — weekday evenings and Saturdays are our busiest hours and the wait can stretch.

Our online booking page lists each barber and their availability, so you can pick the chair as well as the time. If you are not sure who to book, call the shop on (214) 272-9996 and tell us what you are after — we will match you to the right barber.

There is lot parking at 4540 Ross Avenue along with street parking on Ross itself. Bike parking is available, and the entrance to Suite 110 is wheelchair accessible.

Every day. Our barbers work across African-American, Asian, Latino and Caucasian hair, from tight coils to fine straight hair, and they will tell you honestly what will hold and what will not.

A men's haircut is $40 and a skin fade is $35. The haircut and beard together is $50, and the hour-long full service is $60. Smaller services such as an edge up or an eyebrow shape start at $10. The complete list is on our services page.

Yes — gift cards are available at the counter in any amount and can be redeemed against any service or product. Call ahead and we will have one ready for you to collect.

Call the shop as soon as you know. We will hold your chair where we can, though beyond about ten minutes we may need to move you to the next opening so the barbers behind you stay on time.
