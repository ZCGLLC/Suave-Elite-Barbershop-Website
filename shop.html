Styling

Hold, shine and
everything between.

A cut only lasts as long as you can recreate it. These are the finishes we use in the chair, matched to your hair type rather than the label.

Firm Hold Pomade

Water-based, high hold with a natural sheen. Combs through easily, restyles through the day and washes out clean — the workhorse behind most of our fades and side parts.

At the counter All hair types

Matte Styling Clay

Zero shine, serious texture. Builds thickness into finer hair and holds a messy crop or textured quiff in place without looking wet.

At the counter Fine & medium hair

Styling Paste & Cream

Medium, pliable hold for a softer, more natural finish. The right call for curls and waves you want to define rather than freeze.

At the counter Curly & textured

Sea Salt Spray

A pre-style mist that adds grip and body from the root. Works on damp hair before the dryer, or on day-two hair that has gone flat.

At the counter Volume & texture

Hair Tonic & Grooming Oil

A light, classic finish that conditions the scalp and keeps a comb-back neat without any stiffness. Old-school and still unbeaten.

At the counter Light hold

Hair Spray & Finishing Mist

The last step before you leave the chair. Locks a style in place through a Dallas summer without leaving residue behind.

At the counter Finishing

Beard care

A good beard is
fed, not just cut.

Shaping is half the work. The other half happens at home, every morning, with three things that take under a minute.

Beard Oil

Softens coarse growth and stops the itch underneath. Three or four drops worked into the skin, not just the hair, right after a shower.

At the counter Daily use

Beard Balm

Conditioning with a light hold. Tames the strays, adds shape to a longer beard, and keeps the shape your barber gave it.

At the counter Shape & condition

Beard Brush & Comb

Boar bristle to distribute oil and train the direction of growth, plus a fine-tooth comb for the moustache and the detail work.

At the counter Tools

Beard Wash & Conditioner

Cleans without stripping the natural oils the way body soap does. Two or three times a week is plenty.

At the counter Cleansing

Moustache Wax

Firm, high-hold wax for keeping the moustache out of your mouth and the ends exactly where you set them.

At the counter Strong hold

Beard Care Kit

Oil, balm and a brush together in one box. The simplest way to start taking care of a beard properly — and a straightforward gift.

At the counter Gift ready

Shave & skin

What we finish
the shave with.

Aftershave & Balm

Calms the skin, closes the pores and keeps the redness down after a razor pass. Alcohol-free balms available for sensitive skin.

At the counter Post-shave

Shave Cream & Lather

Rich, slow-drying lather that lets the blade glide instead of drag. Brush-applied in the chair, tub or tube to take home.

At the counter Pre-shave

Talc & Neck Powder

The finishing dust that takes the clippings off and leaves the neck cool and dry. The smell most men remember from their first barbershop.

At the counter Finishing

Hair & scalp

Care between
appointments.

Shampoo & Conditioner

Sulphate-free cleansing that will not strip colour or dry out the scalp, with a conditioner matched to your hair texture.

At the counter All hair types

Scalp Treatment

Tonics and serums for dryness, flaking and thinning at the crown. Ask your barber to look before you buy — not everyone needs it.

At the counter Targeted care

Durags & Wave Caps

Silk and velvet durags plus stocking caps to hold a pattern overnight and keep waves and braids laid down.

At the counter Textured hair
