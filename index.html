Our Story

Where Craft Meets Character

Suave Elite Barbershop is a Latino-owned, family-oriented establishment that has earned its reputation as one of Dallas's finest grooming destinations.

Located in the heart of Old East Dallas, we blend modern aesthetics with timeless barbering traditions. Our skilled artisans specialize in precision fades, intricate designs, and the art of the straight razor shave — delivering results that exceed expectations, every visit.

  • Expert fades & lineups for all hair types
  • Traditional hot towel & straight razor shaves
  • Wheelchair accessible · LGBTQ+ friendly space
  • Premium grooming products available in-shop
