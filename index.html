The house

Quiet luxury. Uncompromising craft.

Suave Elite is a modern, family-oriented barbershop in Old East Dallas, known for a calm room, meticulous lineups, and barbers who listen before they cut. Clients come for high fades, skin fades, classic gentlemen’s cuts, and traditional straight-razor work — then stay for the consistency.

The shop is Latino-owned, wheelchair accessible, and built for a mixed clientele: African-American, Asian, curly, and children’s hair are all part of the daily book. Appointments are recommended. Walk-ins are welcomed when time allows. Credit and debit are accepted; parking is available on the street, in a private lot, and with validation.
