The Suave standard

A barbershop built on
detail, not hurry.

Every chair at Suave Elite is run by a barber who treats the consultation as seriously as the cut. We listen first, work methodically, and finish with the details most shops skip — the line, the neck, the hot towel, the mirror check.

The room is clean, the music is right, and the clientele is as diverse as Dallas itself. Whether you are here before a boardroom morning or bringing your son in for his first fade, you get the same standard.

  • Consultation before every cut
  • Straight razor line ups
  • Hot towel & steam finish
  • All hair types & textures
  • Sanitised tools between clients
  • On-time appointments
