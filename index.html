The House of Sharp

Not just a haircut.
An experience.

Based in Old East Dallas, Suave Elite Barbershop is a modern, family-oriented shop with a relaxed aesthetic and an uncompromising standard of craft. Our resident barbers specialize in men's haircuts, fades and styling, traditional straight razor shaves and hot towel service — and we are true beard experts.

Every chair is held by a seasoned professional with years of experience and a loyal book of clients. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll leave with a clean, sharp look — and a reason to come back.
