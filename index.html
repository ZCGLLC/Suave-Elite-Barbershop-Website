Our Story

A Modern Barbershop, Built on Old-School Craft

Tucked into the Old East Dallas quarter on Ross Avenue, Suave Elite Barbershop was founded on a simple idea: every client deserves a sharp, precise cut and a genuinely great experience — every single visit. What started as a small neighborhood shop has grown into one of Dallas's most trusted names in men's grooming, with a full roster of barbers trained in everything from razor-sharp skin fades to traditional straight-razor shaves.

Our chairs welcome everyone — from first fades to first dates, from board meetings to big nights out. Walk-ins are always welcome, though we recommend booking ahead to guarantee your preferred barber and time. Inside, you'll find a clean, modern space, a curated playlist, and a team that takes as much pride in the details as you do.

  • Family-owned & locally operated in the heart of Dallas
  • Skilled, detail-obsessed barbers for every hair type and texture
  • Clean, modern lounge with free Wi-Fi and on-site parking
  • Walk-ins welcome — appointments recommended for peak hours
